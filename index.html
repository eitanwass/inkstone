Free · No account · Runs in your browser

Everything a map needs, nothing it doesn't

  • Rooms and walls on a grid

    Everything moves, resizes and snaps to the grid. Draw as much as you want and unleash your creativity!

  • Tokens with conditions

    Medium, Large or Huge, with names and pictures. Mark Prone, Poisoned or Dead, or invent your own conditions!

  • Live with your table

    Share a link and everyone views and edits the same map - live!

  • Measure as you draw

    A ruler in feet or squares, measuring your players' movements to the dot!

  • Bring your own map

    Put a scanned or generated map behind the grid. "Fit to grid" to automatically line it up with the board!

  • Yours to keep - forever

    Your map is saved in your browser as you work. Export it as a PNG or save it as a file to import later on another device. No account required!
