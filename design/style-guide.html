Inkstone style guide

The visual companion to DESIGN.md: what the logo, colour, type, shape and components look like. The values come from style.css and the logo files in design/logo.

Logo

A pen nib that is also a compass needle: ink, pointing north. On a dark tile with cut corners, the same cut as the UI's panels.

Full mark: app corner, anything 24px or larger.

Small-size mark: browser tabs, under 24px. No ring or ticks.

Home-screen icon: opaque square, mark kept in the central 80%.

Alternate (not used): the cairn, kept in case of a change of mind.

Shape and surface

Chamfered panels with a brass hairline along the top edge and a deep soft shadow: the signature shape. The logo tile repeats it.

Radii: 4 (buttons, inputs), 6 (popovers, menus, toasts, icon buttons), 8 (dialogs, modals), pill (Live), 50% (swatches, tokens). Layers: 50 ambient, 100 panels, 1000 popovers, 2000 modals, 3000 toasts.

Icons

20×20 grid, 1.4 stroke, round caps, currentColor. Muted on dark, primary on hover, gold when active.
